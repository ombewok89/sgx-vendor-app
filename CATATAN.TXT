# 2. VISUAL DIRECTION (DARK THEME PREMIUM)

==================================================

Gunakan **dark theme enterprise premium** dengan karakter:

* deep navy base (bukan hitam pekat)
* midnight blue surface layering
* subtle indigo depth
* controlled contrast
* soft glow accent (bukan neon berlebihan)
* data-first readability

### COLOR SYSTEM (DARK + PREMIUM)

**Base Colors**

* Background: #0B1220 (Deep Navy Black)
* Surface 1: #111A2E
* Surface 2: #16213A
* Surface 3: #1C2A4A

**Primary Accent**

* Royal Blue: #3B82F6
* Indigo: #6366F1

**Secondary Accent**

* Purple Soft: #8B5CF6
* Cyan Data Accent: #22D3EE (very limited use)

**Semantic Colors**

* Success: #22C55E (Emerald)
* Warning: #F59E0B (Amber)
* Critical: #EF4444 (Red)
* Info: #38BDF8 (Sky Blue)

**Neutral Text**

* Primary Text: #E5E7EB
* Secondary Text: #9CA3AF
* Muted Text: #6B7280

> Semua warna harus digunakan secara **fungsional, bukan dekoratif**
